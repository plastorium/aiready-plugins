# What matters by business type

AIReady projects carry a product type. The questions worth asking, the
competitors that count and the features that matter differ by type. Ask only
what the type needs and the person has not already given; propose values.

| Type                                              | Identity that matters                                          | Competitors that count                                    | Features usually compared                                                                      | Do not ask for                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Local, storefront** (dentist, restaurant, shop) | name, address/city, category, hours, phone                     | same category, same city or neighbourhood                 | reviews and rating, local listings, hours/NAP accuracy, local content                          | national chains unless the person names them   |
| **Service-area** (plumber, cleaner, contractor)   | name, service area (cities or radius), services, no storefront | businesses serving the same area for the same service     | reviews, service pages per area, listings, licences/trust signals                              | a street address the business does not publish |
| **Ecommerce**                                     | brand, product categories, markets shipped to, languages       | brands and retailers answering the same product questions | product/category content, structured data, marketplace presence, reviews of products           | a physical location, opening hours             |
| **B2B software / services**                       | product name, category, buyer segment, markets, languages      | products AI answers compare it with for the same job      | comparison and category content, documentation, third-party reviews (G2-type sites), citations | location, hours, phone, local directories      |

- A directory, marketplace or publisher that appears in answers is a
  **channel**. That is an extra role, not an exclusion: a marketplace can
  also compete for the same customer job (common in ecommerce). Say which
  roles a name plays in this context and let the person confirm the cohort;
  never drop it on the channel label alone.
- One business can be several contexts (a plumber in two cities, software sold
  in two languages). Competitors are relative to a context; say which.
- If the type itself is wrong (a service-area business saved as a storefront),
  correcting it is the first proposal, because it changes which features and
  competitors apply.
