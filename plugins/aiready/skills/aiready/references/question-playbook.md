# Question playbook

The questions people actually ask, the skill that owns each, the tools that
answer it today and the limit to say out loud. Tools are discovered at
runtime; this table is a snapshot. Follow
each result's `next` hints rather than chaining further reads by default.

| Question                                                                       | Skill                  | Tools (in order)                                                                                                                                                 | Say plainly                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Do AI chats mention my brand, and for which questions?                         | review-my-performance  | `visibility` (summary, then engines and answers)                                                                                                                 | tools label mentions and list positions, not "recommended"; report a mention rate with its denominator per engine and source (web vs API)                                            |
| Where do I stand against competitors in my niche?                              | compare-competitors    | `compare`, `visibility` (answers)                                                                                                                                | the ranking covers the named cohort only; nulls are unknown                                                                                                                          |

Is it free?                                                 | aiready                | `whoami`, `measure_plan` (if a business exists)                                                                                                                  | explain what uses quota or credits and how it fits their plan (reading and setup use none); plan and price details are on the web app; never sell                                    |

## Iterative journey

AIReady improves the picture in bounded rounds: initial business evidence →
first discovery → competitor and topic refinement → wider question coverage →
improvements → repeated measurement. Each round stops at the budget the person
authorized. When a round ends, say what it covered, what was deliberately
left out ("directions not yet explored") and which next round would close the
biggest gap — as a suggestion, not a started job.

## Questions worth asking (at most three, only when they change something)

| Ask only when…                                                     | Example, with a proposed value                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| several businesses match and the answer differs between them       | "Rose City Plumbing (Portland) or Rose City Drains (Salem)?"              |
| the business identity is uncertain before a first analysis         | "I'll treat this as a residential plumber in Portland — correct?"         |
| a measurement's scope choice changes cost or what can be concluded | "Standard scan of the saved 24 questions, one of 3 remaining — go ahead?" |
| a competitor cohort choice changes the conclusion                  | "Compare with the 5 local plumbers, or include national chains?"          |

Do not ask for location, phone, hours, directory profiles or competitors
unless the business type and the question need them (see
understand-my-business/references/business-types.md).
